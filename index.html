THE BIG PICTURE

How it works

This prototype uses a simulated occupancy feed. Every 15 seconds, it adjusts illustrative parking counts to demonstrate how a real-time dashboard could behave.

01 Collect In production: counters, sensors, cameras, or an authorized UTSA feed.
02 Calculate Available spots = lot capacity − occupied spots.
03 Display Keep spot counts, progress bars, and availability statuses updated.
